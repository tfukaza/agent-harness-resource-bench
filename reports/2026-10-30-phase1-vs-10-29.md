# Phase-1 rerun, 2026-10-30 versus 2026-10-29

The 2026-10-29 phase-1 run was taken on a host that was swapping (about 3 GiB of a 4 GiB swap file in use,
Chrome, ChatGPT and Figma resident). macOS compresses and pages out idle pages under pressure, so summed RSS
over a process tree reads low and then jumps when the process wakes. The 2026-10-30 rerun was taken on a
quiet host and is the run to cite. Same code, same pinned versions, same scenarios and repeats.

## Host state

| run | available at start | swap at start | min host free during any trial | pageouts per trial | preflight warning |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 2026-10-29 | not recorded | not recorded | not recorded | not recorded | n/a |
| 2026-10-30 | 6.6 GiB of 16 | 258 MiB of 1 GiB | 6.07 GiB | 0 to 2.4 MB | none |

Swap stayed at 258 MiB for the whole run. Host pressure instrumentation was added for this rerun; see the
"Host pressure" section of the report.

## What changed

The sum of trial-to-trial RSS spreads (max minus min across repeats, over every scenario, harness and
checkpoint column) fell from 1006 MiB to 486 MiB. Every spread that the old run attributed to a harness and
that was really host noise collapsed:

| scenario | harness | column | old median MiB | old spread MiB | new median MiB | new spread MiB |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| tool-loop | vercel | turn 50 | 74.1 | 61.4 | 135.9 | 2.4 |
| tool-loop | vercel | turn 30 | 101.4 | 67.5 | 125.2 | 1.5 |
| tool-loop | langgraph | turn 20 | 103.4 | 51.3 | 103.3 | 0.2 |
| tool-loop | codex | turn 20 | 71.7 | 84.0 | 58.4 | 0.2 |
| context-ramp | codex | init | 54.6 | 67.8 | 54.4 | 0.7 |
| tool-loop | claude | turn 10 | 359.7 | 33.5 | 360.1 | 13.7 |

Two conclusions from the old report were wrong in direction, not just magnitude. Vercel's tool-loop RSS does
not fall from 110 MiB to 74 MiB over 50 turns; it climbs steadily from 116 MiB to 136 MiB. Codex's per-turn
RSS is not 52 MiB with 80 MiB swings; it is a flat 57 to 61 MiB.

## Spreads that remain, and why

- **codex `init` (tool-loop 67 MiB, concurrency 47 MiB).** The initialized phase is sampled while the first
  `codex exec` child starts, so the median of the last ten samples depends on whether the native binary has
  spawned yet. This is the sampling caveat already in the README, not host noise; the turn checkpoints for
  the same trials are stable to 0.3 MiB.
- **adk context-ramp (9 to 11 MiB on every checkpoint).** Trial 0 sat at 110 MiB at every checkpoint, trials
  1 and 2 at 99 MiB, each stable to 0.1 MiB within the trial. A constant per-process offset like this is an
  allocator or import-order difference, not paging. Worth a repeat with more trials if ADK becomes a target.
- **claude context-ramp peak (46 MiB).** All three trials fail at the 4 MiB checkpoint with "Prompt is too
  long"; peak depends on how far into the failing request the CLI got before exiting.

## Statuses

Unchanged: 90 success, 3 completed_with_compaction (codex compacts the 4 MiB history), 3 failed (claude
rejects the 4 MiB history). Validation passed with 0 errors and the same 6 warnings in both runs.
